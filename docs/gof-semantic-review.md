# Goblet of Fire retrieval pass — 2026-10-11

The policy-2 inventory represents GoF1–GoF37 with 129 character records
(117 people/other speaking referents and 12 animals), 61 places, 66 objects
and 37 identified spells: 293 records. Selected paired passages and literal
mentions represent every chapter. This is a curated inventory, not every noun
or an independently reviewed full-book reading.

## Attribution and casting

Crouch Junior and the real Alastor Moody have separate human IDs. The apparent
teacher's classroom demonstrations, Map summons and admitted interventions
belong to the son. His apparent-name aliases support earlier-chapter retrieval,
but the record exposes the later revelation. A chapter filter never certifies
what a viewpoint character knows. The captive Auror has no invented classroom
casting rows. Father/son Crouch and the adult/pupil Crabbe, Goyle and Nott
referents likewise stay separate. Rita's beetle form is the same human reporter;
Peter retains his rat aliases without another animal record.

There are 75 casting/practice/reported-event rows and five qualified class
reports: Crouch Senior's Memory Charm, three attributed Imperius uses, and the
impostor's stunning of Fleur. Class reports do not establish an uttered formula.
Grouped repeated practice and simultaneous attacks are not physical cast
counts. No occurrence has proven deliberate non-verbal casting.

The Goblet manipulation links the impostor's GoF17 explanation of the named
Confundus class to his GoF35 admission that he entered Harry as a fourth school's
sole entrant. This is attributed method evidence, not an observed casting or
proof of an exact formula. The earlier PS Killing Curse identification remains
anchored to GoF14 without rewriting what PS characters could know.

## Exclusions and objects

| Observation | Decision |
|---|---|
| Green light without a named spell, caster or identifiable event | Do not diagnose a Killing Curse from colour alone. |
| Ferret transformation, Arthur's later repairs, wine fountain at wand inspection, Wormtail's new hand | Unidentified effects do not acquire a familiar formula. The hand has an object/property record. |
| Dragon handlers' collective Stunners; anonymous Ministry volleys | Do not invent individual practitioners from a crowd. |
| Potion consumption, gillyweed, sweets, Portkey/Cup travel, Goblet selection | Substances or object operations, not casts by their users. |
| Animagus changes, Veela displays, phoenix healing, Dementor Kiss | Abilities/creature acts; the resulting information can still matter to identity and evidence records. |
| Drought/other suggested methods without an actual identifiable casting | Theory alone is not a casting row. |
| Shield, Banishing, Confundus, breathing bubble, jar protection | Named classes qualify where an actual human use is established; no unattested Protego, Depulso or Confundo formula is imported. |
| Linked wands producing echoes | Separate from Amos's deliberate last-spell inspection; neither event proves routine resurrection. |
| Ascendio, Aguamenti or a trunk-opening formula supplied by adaptations | Absent from this novel inventory. Dumbledore opens the trunk with keys. |

The Goblet selector, Cup Portkey, golden egg and seven-lock trunk are distinct
objects. The Map's shared Crouch name initially misleads Harry; the record does
not turn it into a father/son discriminator for him. The real Moody's eye and
leg are on his bedside table in GoF36 and fitted at the GoF37 feast. Ordinary
clothing, containers and publications remain searchable without becoming
enchanted. A magical substance inside a flask does not enchant its container.

Hermione's GoF28 electronics explanation is recorded as testimony, with a
constraint on Muggle electrical substitutes. It is not an experimental device
specification or a rule against all mechanical equipment. The captured son's
GoF36 Kiss makes fresh questioning unavailable; the Veritaserum record does
not imply a way to restore a soul or convert testimony into official acceptance.

## Edition and review limits

The indexed Hungarian edition uses Invito, Piroinitio, Baziteo, Carbunculus,
Orchidessis, Relaxo, Stimula, Annullate and Obstructo. It calls gillyweed
varangydudva, the optical device omniszkóp, the hill Hermelindomb and the
detector Subrosa-szenzor. Descriptive labels for unnamed rooms/equipment are
editorial labels, not claims of printed headwords.

Original EPUB XHTML confirms seven Adava spellings and zero Avada spellings
in Hungarian GoF14. This edition distinction is preserved; no universal spelling
claim is made. GoF9 has EN Prior Incantato and HU Priori incantatem for Amos's
inspection. The separate twin-core effect occurs in GoF34 and is named/explained
in GoF36. Hungarian GoF31 uses Expelliarmus, while GoF34 uses Capitulatus.

The inventory stores 381 contextual evidence pairs and 943 literal-mention
pairs. Repeated locators are not distinct passages or independent readings.
Coverage states remain `partial`; the completed retrieval pass has a narrower
scope. Finalized candidate counters count records, not every excluded observation
listed above. Validation and writing-evaluation results are recorded in the
[GoF writing pilot](gof-writing-pilot.md), with first outputs, retrieval failures
and unresolved editorial limits preserved separately from mechanical checks.
