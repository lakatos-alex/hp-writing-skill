# Seven core books: 1.0 milestone

The seven novels now have characters, identified human spells, places and notable
objects under the same policy-2 evidence and coverage contract. This completes
the first ingestion milestone. It is a curated writing reference, not an
exhaustive account of every noun, cast or interpretation in the novels.

## Source decisions

HBP includes the Prince's authorship reveal, positive silent-casting evidence,
spell and counter-spell identities, potion effects, cursed objects and the
distinction between the genuine and substitute lockets. Silent Body-Bind
casting at the tower has an explicit subsequent identification anchor. Harry's
spoken Shield Charm in class does not satisfy the silent-casting instruction.
His HBP18 Levicorpus wording differs between the two editions, so vocalisation
is retained per language rather than asserting silence in both.

DH includes the Hallows, wand possession and allegiance, the Horcrux hunt,
the Ministry and bank intrusions, the battle and the epilogue. A whisper is
spoken casting. Hermione's alteration of her parents' identities does not
acquire an invented Obliviate formula. The café attackers' memory erasure is a
separate named event. Fiendfyre has an identified caster and named effect, with
no invented incantation. Unnamed attacks, object operation, potion use and
creature abilities are outside the spell results. Named failed attempts remain.

Source review also separates a successful spell from a failed wider objective:
Harry produces water in the cave, but the protection prevents Dumbledore from
drinking it. The first revival attempt has no visible effect; the repeated
attempt precedes Dumbledore waking, without restoring his full strength.

The Hungarian DH transcription has missing accents, damaged spacing and
inconsistent wording. Paired locators remain useful retrieval keys; they do not
certify spelling. DH results retain the warning and label provenance, and the
Hungarian advisory scan offers no DH replacements. Earlier reliable labels may
be reused; new editorial labels require edition verification. Supplementary
names are not treated as printed novel evidence.

Cross-book retrieval preserves book records and adds shared identity links.
Examples include the Put-Outer/Deluminator and the drawing-room/Slytherin
locket. Their later names and properties do not become earlier character
knowledge. Draco's copied communication coins remain distinct from the DA's
original coins; portraits, disguises, substitute objects and broken parts are
not indiscriminately merged.

## Changes supported by the earlier writing comparison

The [preserved comparison](ootp-writing-review.md) used two broad-context
briefs, eight fresh writing agents under four resource conditions and two
independent judges with conditions hidden. Every draft, including the bare
model drafts, passed the stated factual checks. Both judges preferred primary
book access over the bare model on each brief; intermediate conditions had
mixed preferences. Those results do not establish general writing superiority.

The observed Prince and non-verbal lookup gaps were missing HBP/DH data.
The new inventories and two focused fact cards address those gaps. Existing
viewpoint guidance already covers the reported sightline concern, so no new
mandatory writing workflow was added. The router now describes the common
seven-book interface instead of accumulating another paragraph for each book.
The existing results supported retrieval development; they do not measure the
effect of this new data or of cross-book retrieval.

## Verification status and next work

The interrupted files were recovered and parsed; incomplete placeholder
categories were replaced from preserved source work. Inventory construction
requires paired locators, resolved human casters and all chapter anchors.
Public data contains factual names, short locators and original paraphrases;
books and full-text research stay outside the distribution.

The user explicitly deferred new tests for this milestone. No new writing
evaluation, local automated-suite run or installation smoke test is claimed.
The earlier five-book checks are historical evidence. GitHub's existing push
workflow may run automatically; its observed status is separate from local
verification and literary evaluation. Static file, version and archive
inspection is part of release preparation, not a writing-quality result.

Next work is a fresh held-out comparison if requested, independent reading
and editorial review, and supported reference corrections. Human Hungarian
editorial review remains pending. No additional-book list has been selected.

status, then complete its typed inventory using the same contract.
