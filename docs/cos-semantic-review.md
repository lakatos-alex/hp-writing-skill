## Objects, places and edition observations

Ordinary objects include Colin's Muggle camera, Harry's spectacles, the mirror
found beside the petrified pupils, Harry's sock and reading titles. Moving
photographs are indexed separately from the camera. The mirror's ownership is
not established; its protective use is Harry's interpretation. Borgin's Hand
of Glory description and the opal necklace's warning remain attributed
testimony. `demonstrated` can denote an explicit property description or report,
not necessarily a witnessed test of every advertised feature.

The two cabinets are separate records. CoS does not establish their later
paired transport mechanism. Gryffindor's sword has no imported goblin-forging
or venom-absorption property. The single-handed Burrow kitchen clock is not
conflated with the later family-status clock.

Checked Wiki object-classification pointers include the

[Invisibility Booster](https://harrypotter.fandom.com/wiki/Invisibility_Booster),

The indexed Hungarian edition uses Mallter, Caramel, Celestina Maggica,
Widra St. Capdel, Pótcsont-Rapid, Altatóital, Capitulatus and Exmemoriam.
The twins' Cleansweep Fives are printed as `Jólsep-RS-eket` in the EPUB XHTML
as well as the transcription. The descriptive Hungarian label retains the
Jólsep-R family and the printed RS variant as an alias; it does not silently
repair the suffix to a numeral. Descriptive room and unnamed-object labels are
editorial labels, not claims of printed canonical headwords.

## Review and validation scope

Selected English/Hungarian contexts were checked for referents, translations,
properties and casts; weak locators were tightened when they matched another
wand, classroom or garden substring. Literal term mentions stay separate from
physical presence. Descriptions summarize the whole book: a chapter filter
does not conceal the Riddle reveal or remove later events from the record.
Source hashes and coverage are recorded in the CoS dataset. All chapter
reading states remain `partial`; independent full-book reading and editorial
review were not performed.

The combined PS/CoS inventories contain 501 stored contextual evidence pairs
and 843 literal-mention pairs. These can reuse passages. Counts do not measure
independent reading or unique evidence.
