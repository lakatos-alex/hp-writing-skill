# Prisoner of Azkaban retrieval pass — 2026-10-10

The policy-2 inventory represents PoA1–PoA22 with 89 character records
(78 other character records and 11 separately typed animals), 56 places,
72 notable object types, artefacts and titles, and 14 identified spells:
231 records. It is a scoped retrieval inventory, not a record of every noun
or an independently reviewed full-book reading.

The index has 32 casting, practice or report rows. Grouped practice, repeated
reported uses and simultaneous casting do not count individual physical casts.

unreported rather than acquiring a guessed formula or mechanism.

The lake rescue has one `eventId`, anchored at the PoA21 casting.
`observationEvidence` records the PoA20 view of that same event. It contributes

## Identities, places and objects

Scabbers/Makesz, Peter Pettigrew and Wormtail/Féregfark share one human record.
Sirius and his dog form likewise share a record. Named ordinary animals remain
separately typed. Sirius's gift owl has a descriptive label because its later
Pigwidgeon name is not supplied here. Professor Kettleburn is Ebshont professzor
in the indexed Hungarian edition; a later first name is not added.

Sirius is held in Flitwick's seventh-floor office. The West Tower is a landmark
used to locate that window, rather than the room's name. The two places are
separate records. The Hungarian street is Magnolia köz, and the EPUB prints
Anglesea for the English Anglesey. Descriptive room labels are editorial,
not claims of printed headwords. Godric's Hollow is not carried over from
another book without a PoA naming passage.

Ordinary objects include the servicing kit, clippers, compass, permission
papers and reading titles. Harry's spectacles now have an attested magical
property from Impervius; this does not retroactively change their earlier-book
classification. The Map, Time-Turner, moving photographs, flying brooms and
potions have their own property evidence. A demonstrated status may denote
explicit testimony or description, rather than an independently witnessed
test. Rat tonic, the Lunascope and Harry's failed Confusing Concoction remain
`not-established` for enchantment or demonstrated operation.

Checked Wiki catalogue pointers include the
[Marauder's Map](https://harrypotter.fandom.com/wiki/Marauder%27s_Map),

Hungarian names include Exlukhops, Comikulissimus, Leperex, Észbontó kompót,
Kométa Kettő-Hatvan, Bűvös Bizsere and Drubli Legjobb Fúvógumija. Ron's
Honeydukes description pairs Chocoballs with csokipocak; it does not identify
the separately mentioned Cauldron Cakes. The editions describe different fruit
fillings. The third-year Standard Book title has no printed grade numeral in
the checked Hungarian purchase passage. Names and meaningful edition
differences are retained without repairing the text from another edition.

## Review and validation scope

Selected English/Hungarian contexts were reviewed for identities, properties,
cast practitioners, outcomes and edition names. Literal mentions are checked
separately from presence. A chapter filter selects whole-book records and can
return later revelations; it is not a strict scene-knowledge cutoff.

PoA stores 273 contextual evidence pairs and 537 literal-mention pairs.
The combined three-book reference stores 774 contextual pairs and 1380 literal
pairs. They can reuse passages and do not count unique evidence or independent
readings. All 22 chapter reading states remain `partial`; independent full-book
semantic reading and editorial review are incomplete. Source hashes are stored
with coverage. Mechanical validation does not certify literary completeness.
