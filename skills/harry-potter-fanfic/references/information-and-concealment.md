# Information, truth and concealment

Use when a mystery, security arrangement or relationship depends on who can discover what. The central distinction is between an event, a trace of it, an interpretation and a decision to believe.

## Information surfaces

| Surface | Chapter route | What to establish in a scene |
|---|---|---|
| Spoken testimony | PoA10, PoA18–19; DH25, DH28 | Speaker's access, agenda, uncertainty and audience |
| Memory in a Pensieve | HBP17, HBP23; DH33 | Owner, alterations, selection and viewer interpretation |
| Legilimency | OotP24 | Conditions, resistance and interpretive limits |
| Marauder's Map | PoA10, PoA17–18 | Holder, visible location, identity clues and what the observer can conclude |
| Enchanted coins | OotP19; HBP27 | Who has a linked object, who sets information and who may intercept it |
| A portrait | DH33 | Where it can observe, which portrait receives information and who trusts it |
| Newspaper or interview | GoF18, GoF24; OotP25–26 | Access, editing, distribution and reputation |
| Trace | CoS2; DH4 | Detection near an underage person versus identification of the caster |
| Taboo | DH22 | A spoken trigger and the later wartime mechanism, not a timeless property of the name |

These routes are candidates for verification. Do not infer that any surface supplies complete, unambiguous truth.

## Secret architecture

Fidelius requires the secret, keeper, disclosure and audience to be specified. PoA10's explanation, DH6's keeper-death consequences and DH25's Shell Cottage exchange should be read together for a rule-dependent plot. Do not import a fanon rule that makes every resident categorically ineligible to keep a home's secret.

Concealment can also depend on ordinary behaviour. A hidden meeting place does not protect members who discuss plans openly, carry recognisable objects, are followed, or trust the wrong person. OotP16–19 and OotP27 connect organisation to surveillance and betrayal.

## Identity is layered

A disguise can alter appearance without granting memories, relationships, credentials or the ability to answer a private question. Polyjuice infiltration needs source material, timing and behavioural knowledge. A familiar name on a Map can contradict appearances without explaining the whole plot.

Track physical identity, presented identity, role and audience belief separately when the story needs them. Fourth-year “Moody” is the real Auror's face and reputation used by another actor.

## Truth procedures

Veritaserum's use in GoF35 is an event to inspect. It does not by itself establish an infallible general system for proving every accusation. A speaker can lack information, a witness can be prevented from testifying, and an institution can refuse an inconvenient conclusion.

Avoid importing unstated antidotes or legal doctrines merely to close every possible loophole. If a new story needs a rule about admissibility or resistance, check the primary source and label remaining invention.

## Scene questions

Who can ask the question? Who is willing to answer? What would make the answer costly? Can the recipient verify it independently? What happens if the truth arrives late? These questions often produce stronger conflict than making a magical information tool arbitrarily fail.
