# Magic engineering

Use for magical inventions, research projects, duels, healing, travel, concealment or a plot that depends on a rule.

## Separate precedent from extension

Start with the required effect and a specific book precedent. Hermione's Protean coins establish linked information updates. They do not establish arbitrary bandwidth, authentication, distance limits or a general network operating system. Those are design decisions for the fanfic.

Use three layers: demonstrated behaviour; stated explanation and speaker; proposed extension. Do not claim that an elegant extension was explicitly established.

## Design card

Adapt these fields to the project's notes:
- Effect, target and expected result.
- Required knowledge, training, material or wand.
- Activation conditions and who can authorise or interrupt it.
- Range, duration and scope where established; open questions where not.
- Failure mode, recovery and what witnesses can observe.
- Cost in time, access, risk, labour or relationships.
- Canon anchors and explicit invented assumptions.

Avoid inventing a universal mana budget just to fill a cost field. Canonical inconvenience may be secrecy, ingredient access, concentration, repair or the consequences of using the tool.

## Systems that need care

**Travel:** distinguish permission, physical possibility and safe execution. Hogwarts restrictions have specific exceptions; house-elves do not obey all wizard limitations. A rescue needs someone who can reach the people, knows where they are and chooses to help.

**Information:** define what is sensed, what is inferred and who receives it. Concealment, false identity and withheld information do not have one universal counterspell.

**Healing:** establish what changed physically and what treatment can affect. Bill's injuries and the Longbottoms' condition show that magic does not imply complete restoration. Emotional reconciliation is a different process.

**Food and supply:** preserve the distinction between producing food from nothing and obtaining, changing or increasing existing food. The Room's tunnel in DH29 is logistics, not an exception allowing conjured meals.

**Contracts:** name the actual mechanism. The Tournament contract and Unbreakable Vow are different examples. Do not invent a complete magical contract law from either.

## Adversarial review of the design

Ask how a competent opponent, a careless user and an unexpected visitor could affect it. If one cheap spell defeats a major obstacle, either use the available solution or establish a supported reason it cannot apply. Avoid adding invisible exceptions only after the reader notices a loophole.

For a new capability, plan how it is learned or discovered, an early limited use, a failure that teaches something, and the later payoff. Keep discovery proportional to the character's expertise and available help. An original character can be skilled without independently reinventing every discipline.

Deliver a coherent proposal or the requested scene. Name unresolved mechanics that affect the plot; leave harmless unknowns alone.
