# Revision studio

Use for structural edits, line edits, proofreading and review. Infer the requested depth from the user; preserve the project's accepted voice and conventions.

## Diagnose the level

A structural problem concerns cause, scene purpose, ordering or missing development. A character problem concerns motive, knowledge or agency. A continuity problem concerns incompatible accepted states. A line problem concerns how existing content reads. Proofreading concerns correctness and consistency.

Repair at the right level. Adding an adjective will not resolve a missing motive; rewriting a plot to make one sentence shorter exceeds a line edit.

## Evidence before intervention

Read the full target and enough surrounding material to understand setup and payoff. Use approved passages as the voice baseline: narrative distance, sentence rhythm, emotional explicitness, humour, dialogue conventions, imagery and recurring patterns.

For review, report concrete findings with locations and effects. Separate demonstrable contradictions from interpretive preferences. If the prose intentionally delays an answer, identify what makes the delay confusing rather than demanding immediate exposition.

## Preserve meaningful complexity

Long sentences, personification, repetition and ornate imagery can be deliberate. A living house may be literally responsive within the AU, a viewpoint impression or a metaphor. Determine which before removing agency or introducing new powers.

Check repetition by function. An object recurring at successive stages may develop a relationship. An identical paragraph copied twice may be an accident. The optional audit tool reports candidates; it does not decide which to cut.

## Line editing

Keep events, motives, uncertainty and clue content fixed unless development is authorised. Remove explanatory duplication only when the action already carries the meaning. Keep distinct dialogue strategies; do not make every speaker concise, therapeutic or witty.

Read transitions for emotional logic. A character's mood should change because something happened, was remembered, was inferred or was chosen. A smooth sentence can still hide an unsupported reversal.

For multilingual work, preserve forms of address, dialogue punctuation and culturally specific rhythms chosen by the project. Do not turn the English reference library into a requirement to write English prose.

## Developmental revision

When broader changes are authorised, add the missing causal work rather than padding. Update the next affected reaction or payoff. Use the continuity workflow for delayed secrets, changed object custody or revised survival.

Compare the saved result to the original target and voice baseline. Measure agreed length, check required beats, and explain material changes. A successful edit improves the requested dimension while preserving what the user values.
