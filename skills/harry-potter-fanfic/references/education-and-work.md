# Education, competence and work

Use for lessons, research, adult expertise, careers, mentoring and institutional routines.

## School as a working institution

PS5's supplies and PS8's classes establish material and organisational needs. PoA7 and PoA12 show teaching through demonstration, practice and adjustment. OotP29–31 connects career aims, qualifications and examination performance. HBP9 shows how a new teacher's expectations change access and classroom incentives.

A subject's existence does not establish a complete syllabus. Ancient Runes, Arithmancy and other studies leave room for invention; label an invented technical curriculum. Avoid claiming that every pupil learns the same advanced spell at a precisely specified age unless the source establishes it.

## Teaching scenes

Give the lesson an objective the pupil can attempt and the teacher can observe. Decide what a mistake reveals: misunderstanding, distraction, fear, an equipment problem or lack of practice. A demonstration can be impressive without automatically transferring competence.

Lupin's Boggart and Patronus teaching can inform supportive instruction. Snape's expertise does not make humiliation pedagogically harmless. Lockhart's reputation does not provide the ability to perform the required intervention. Keep professional knowledge and treatment of learners as different judgments.

Lessons become plot when someone acquires a skill, exposes a fear, changes status, makes a consequential error, or develops a relationship. A classroom scene that only repeats lore can usually be shortened or given a decision.

## Apprenticeship and specialist knowledge

An original adult specialist should have a domain, experience, resources and limits. A healer may need a curse-breaker; a talented student may understand a principle but lack equipment or supervision. Collaboration makes expertise visible through questions, testing and disagreement.

A new magical technology needs research work: a precedent, an assumption, a prototype, a measurable failure, revised design and a use whose limits still matter. Books, materials, time and access can provide costs without invented power meters.

## Careers and qualifications

OotP29 is a useful starting point for career advice, but check the particular occupation and speaker. Distinguish desired career, required qualification, current post and later outcome. A pupil's ambition is not a canonical employment record.

The epilogue establishes Neville as a professor associated with Herbology. Broader career accounts commonly drawn from later franchise material belong in a labelled extension layer if adopted.

## Workplaces beyond school

Use institution-specific pressures:
- Healers: triage, visiting, confidentiality, recovery and resources.
- Ministry employees: authority, procedure, public message and private judgment.
- Shopkeepers: stock, credit, repairs, customer knowledge and supply.
- Researchers: access, evidence, failed trials and credit for discoveries.
- Hospitality workers and households: food, cleaning, scheduling and unequal labour.

These are writing frameworks, not a newly asserted canonical rulebook. Derive the exact magical procedure and institutional facts from relevant passages.

## Scene outcome

After a lesson or work scene, something should be different: ability, confidence, responsibility, trust, access or a practical constraint. Avoid making every scene end with praise. A useful mentor may set a harder boundary or admit that a question exceeds their expertise.
