# Creatures, beings and coexistence

Use for creature encounters, care, nonhuman characters, diplomacy and prejudice. Separate physical capability, social status and individual motive.

## Encounter design

Identify habitat, the character's purpose for entering it, signs of danger and a possible route to withdrawal. A creature need not attack simply because the scene needs excitement. Territorial defence, pain, hunger, captivity and interrupted care create different situations.

Hagrid's enthusiasm is evidence of his affection, not an independent safety assessment. Students and visitors may lack his experience or physical resilience.

## Persons and systems

A helpful nonhuman character has information, preferences and a reason to cooperate. Dobby, Winky and Kreacher cannot stand in for one universal elf psychology. A person's expressed preference matters while the surrounding coercive system can still be criticised.

A goblin negotiation should specify what each side believes payment or a promise means. Avoid making conflict arise solely because one side is mysteriously dishonest.

Centaurs' response to an intrusion has political and territorial context. An urgent human crisis does not automatically confer a right to use another community.

## Care and responsibility

For a dangerous animal, decide who has expertise, who supplies food or medicine, who may be harmed and who bears responsibility for a failure. A rescue does not end when the cage opens: transport, habitat and long-term care can matter.

For lycanthropy, Wolfsbane's reported role is management of the transformed person's mind, not cure. A relationship can improve resources and support without erasing the condition. Do not equate involuntary risk with moral guilt.

## Expansion space

The books leave many ecological, medical and legal details incomplete. Develop consistent proposals when needed, label their status, and avoid claiming a definitive taxonomy or full international regulation. Return to the user's tone: a quiet care scene and a dangerous rescue need different amounts of procedural detail.
