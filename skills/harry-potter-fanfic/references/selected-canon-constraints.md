# Selected canon constraints for writing

A selective manual view of the shared [evidence cards](../data/facts.json), across all seven novels. Read only the entries that affect this scene. These are constraints, not story beats or a complete scene database; the writer decides what people do about them. Chapter anchors locate evidence, not automatic character knowledge. Keep an event, an attributed claim and an interpretation distinct. If a consequential fact is absent, check the [public inventories](book-dossiers.md#typed-inventory-navigation) or local primary context; absence from this selection is not canon absence.

Before drafting, establish only two to four consequential facts: physical capacity, actual supplies, object capability and the information available at this date. During revision, check those facts against the written actions. Avoid turning the prose into a factual briefing. A mistaken proposal may be rejected by another character; narration should not silently endorse it.

## Physical limits and supplies

- **first-camp-injury — DH14 (event).** After the Ministry escape, Ron has lost flesh from his left upper arm through Splinching. Hermione applies Essence of Dittany; he remains weak and unwell. DH14 concerns the upper arm, not a leg, forearm or collarbone. Stopping the bleeding does not establish restored strength or fitness for an excursion. The English anatomy is decisive because the Hungarian transcription omits precision.

- **first-camp-provisions — DH14 (event).** Hermione packed no food because she expected them to return to Grimmauld Place. The first evening's meal consists of gathered wild mushrooms cooked in a billycan. This is the first camping day after the Ministry escape, not every later camp. Storage capacity and prior planning do not establish a stocked food cache. An invented incidental possession must not erase this shortage.

- **food-exception — DH15 (testimony).** Hermione explains that food cannot be produced from nothing, but existing food can be summoned, transformed or increased. Do not invent the other four exceptions or unbounded yields as established rules.

## Objects and information routes

- **mirror-desire — PS12 (testimony).** Dumbledore explains that the Mirror shows deepest desire, not reliable knowledge or truth. Do not use the image as a prophecy or an objective compatibility test.

- **map-geographic-scope — PoA10 (event).** The Marauder's Map displays Hogwarts routes and moving named occupants, rather than mapping arbitrary surrounding country. PoA10 establishes the school map. Knowing an occupant's location does not establish motive, danger or a safe woodland foraging route. Activation is object operation, not evidence of a new spell cast.

- **beaded-bag-storage — DH9 (event).** Hermione's beaded bag has expanded storage for possessions including clothes, books and camping equipment. Expanded storage does not generate provisions, certify that an item was packed, or make an ordinary stored object magical. Check contents against the particular scene; finding an item can take effort.

- **deluminator-discovery — DH7, DH19 (event).** Ron receives the light-taking Deluminator in DH7. In DH19 he reports hearing Hermione through it and following a guiding light back to the others. The later guiding discovery is not knowledge Ron has before leaving the group. Earlier scenes may use the demonstrated light-taking/restoring function, but should not invent unrestricted positioning, tracking or food-finding powers. The object is not itself an identified human spell cast.

- **protean-coins — OotP19 (testimony).** Hermione links imitation Galleons so changed meeting details on Harry's coin update the others and make them warm. This supports a specific messaging mechanism, not an unlimited internet.

- **neville-wrapper — OotP23 (event).** Alice gives Neville an empty gum wrapper during a hospital visit and he thanks her. The object's relational meaning is interpretation; do not use it as proof of recovered memory.

## Knowledge, testimony and trust

- **diary-coercion — CoS17 (testimony).** Riddle describes manipulating Ginny through the diary and making her act without initial awareness. Riddle is an antagonist explaining his exploitation; keep speaker and coercion visible.

- **trace-misattribution — CoS2 (event).** The Ministry's letter attributes a Hover Charm at Harry's home to underage wrongdoing after Dobby's visit. Detection near a child is not proof of who cast the spell.

- **prince-spell-authorship — HBP28 (testimony).** Snape identifies himself as the Half-Blood Prince and claims authorship of the spells Harry has used from the annotated textbook. This identity is revealed at HBP28. An earlier ownership note or successful spell does not give Harry that knowledge. Authorship does not make every annotation safe.

- **altered-memory — HBP17 (testimony).** Dumbledore identifies Slughorn's displayed memory as tampered with to conceal shameful information. A shown memory still needs provenance and interpretation.

- **grimmauld-refuge-risk — DH14 (testimony).** Hermione reports that Yaxley followed her escape into the Fidelius protection. The trio treats Grimmauld Place as unsafe to return to; Harry regrets losing that refuge and Kreacher's meal. The disclosure is Hermione's explanation and the trio's risk assessment, not witnessed proof of what Yaxley subsequently did. The immediate abandoned refuge is Grimmauld Place. Returning for supplies requires accounting for the exposure risk.

- **taboo-disclosure — DH20 (testimony).** In DH20 Ron explains the name-triggered Taboo to Harry and Hermione; Harry's question shows he did not already understand it. Earlier reluctance to say the name is not the same as knowing this mechanism. Do not give first-camp characters the later explanation solely because the author knows it.

## Learned competence and obligations

- **unlocking-has-limits — PS16 (event).** Hermione's Alohomora attempt fails on the protected door in the winged-key chamber. A successful earlier unlocking does not establish that the charm opens every lock or protection. A failed attempt can be a genuine constraint rather than a reason to invent a stronger unnamed spell.

- **patronus-training — PoA12 (testimony).** Lupin teaches Harry to focus intensely on a happy memory and warns that many qualified wizards struggle. Training success does not guarantee performance under real Dementor pressure.

- **non-verbal-casting-evidence — HBP9 (event).** In the sixth-year Defence exercise, Hermione repels Neville's jinx silently. Harry shouts his successful Shield Charm and fails the instruction to cast without speaking. Silence requires positive evidence and an identified spell. A whispered formula is spoken; omitted words alone do not establish non-verbal casting. Classroom success is not universal battle competence.

- **tournament-contract — GoF17 (testimony).** Adults insist that the selected champions must compete under a binding magical contract. The books do not specify a universal soul-loss or death penalty for nonparticipation.

- **dobby-wages — GoF21 (testimony).** Dobby describes negotiated pay of one Galleon a week and one day off a month, below Dumbledore's offer. One elf's negotiated preference does not establish universal elf wishes.
