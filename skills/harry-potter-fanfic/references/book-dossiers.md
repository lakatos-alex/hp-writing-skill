# Book dossiers

These dossiers organise the seven novels as a source of narrative mechanisms, character changes and world constraints. They are original analysis with chapter navigation, not exhaustive chapter summaries or substitutes for the text.

| Book | Read for | Dossier |
|---|---|---|
| PS | Discovery through activity, belonging, distributed competence | [Philosopher's Stone](book-ps.md) |
| CoS | Prejudice, false suspicion, coercive intimacy and identity | [Chamber of Secrets](book-cos.md) |
| PoA | Conflicting history, unreliable accusation, fear and reinterpretation | [Prisoner of Azkaban](book-poa.md) |
| GoF | Public spectacle, coerced participation, hidden intervention and denial | [Goblet of Fire](book-gof.md) |
| OotP | Institutional pressure, peer organisation, isolation and information cost | [Order of the Phoenix](book-ootp.md) |
| HBP | Incomplete memory, expertise, parallel secret projects and preparation | [Half-Blood Prince](book-hbp.md) |
| DH | Supply, trust, custody, competing quests and costly choice | [Deathly Hallows](book-dh.md) |

The [chapter catalogue](../data/chapters.json) covers all 199 anchors. Topic occurrences are mechanically counted exact terms, not evidence that a chapter proves a proposition. The dossiers combine selected passage verification with broader chapter-based interpretation; see [coverage](coverage.md).

For an ordinary chapter, choose a dossier only if its particular mechanism helps. For a requested series study, compare all seven and return to complete chapters for the decisive evidence.
