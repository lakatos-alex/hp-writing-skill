# Era, knowledge, and divergence ledger
A character may exist in the books without being alive, available or informed at the fanfic's chosen date.

## Narrative eras
| Anchor | What changes | Mistake to catch |
|---|---|---|
| PS1–7 | Harry enters a world he does not understand | Giving eleven-year-old Harry later war knowledge or effortless magical literacy. |
| CoS17–18 | Diary destroyed; Ginny survives; Dobby freed | Treating everyone as already knowing the full Horcrux mechanism. |
| PoA18–22 | The trio learns the betrayal history; Sirius escapes | Turning private knowledge into universal public exoneration. |
| GoF32–37 | Cedric dies; Voldemort returns; denial begins | Confusing reader certainty with official acknowledgment. |
| OotP35–38 | Sirius dies; the Ministry acknowledges Voldemort | Resetting Harry's grief after one comforting conversation. |
| HBP27–30 | Dumbledore dies; Harry prepares to leave school | Treating Dumbledore as a living post-war headmaster without an AU. |
| DH23–24 | Dobby dies after the manor rescue | Summoning him alive later without a divergence. |
| DH31–36 | Fred, Remus and Tonks die; Voldemort is defeated | Writing the full earlier ensemble in a canon post-war gathering. |
| DH epilogue | Adult family outcomes and school departure | Importing every later interview or stage-play fact into a seven-book-only baseline. |

The age of wizarding majority is seventeen, but track actual ages for relationships, schooling and legal rules rather than using one label as an answer to every question.

## Knowledge ledger template
For every mystery or secret, keep: **fact; who knows; how learned; who suspects; false belief; disclosure cost; next revealing event**.
Example: after a disguised intruder is exposed, a student may know the imposture without knowing the entire conspiracy. Avoid retrospectively making every earlier conversation knowingly deceptive on both sides.

## Source hierarchy
1. The user's explicit premise and established story decisions define this fanfic.
2. Seven-book evidence supplies the default when compatible with that premise.
3. Film, interviews, reference sites, stage works and other fanfics are separate sources; identify any adoption.
4. Your interpretation and inventions should be marked as such when recorded.

Preserve deliberate divergences. If adults from incompatible canonical timelines share a school, record survival and age assumptions rather than silently killing someone or restarting the story.

## Change propagation
For each AU decision ask:
- Whose survival, job, home or relationship changes?
- Which later information no longer arrives through the original event?
- Which institution or villain has a different opportunity?
- Which emotional consequence should still exist, and which must be rewritten?
- What remains intentionally unresolved?

A survival AU can keep loss, danger and accountability through different events. Do not mechanically preserve consequences that depended on the removed death.

## What the books leave open
Do not invent exact post-war career dates, numerical power rankings, complete course syllabi or a permanent legal code and label them canon. The epilogue supplies a narrow view. Use plausible proposals, including competing possibilities, when a user's future-era story needs more.

## Revision impact checks

Use the existing project records rather than creating another ledger. Separate the date of an event from the date each witness learns about it. Book knowledge available to the author is not automatically available to the viewpoint character. For changed AU events, trace the information route as well as survival: who now delivers the warning, keeps the object, holds authority, or makes the next decision? Record a missing route as unresolved instead of silently supplying convenient omniscience. A public announcement establishes disclosure, not universal belief or institutional agreement.
