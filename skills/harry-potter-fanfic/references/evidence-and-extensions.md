# Evidence, authority and extensions

The seven novels are the complete primary canon baseline for this skill. “Primary” describes authority; it does not make every statement by a character objectively true.

## Evidence types

| Type | Meaning | Record |
|---|---|---|
| Event | An action or outcome presented in the narrative | Scene, participants, chapter and scope |
| Testimony | A character, document or embedded account makes a claim | Speaker/source, access, possible bias and later correction |
| Interpretation | A reading of patterns, motives or craft | Supporting anchors and plausible alternatives |
| Extension | Material adopted from another franchise medium or external research | Exact source and adoption boundary |
| Invention / AU | A proposed or accepted change for the fanfic | Decision, consequences and acceptance status |
| Unresolved | Evidence does not settle the question | What is known and what remains open |

A Dumbledore explanation can be authoritative within the scene and still have limited scope. A Riddle account can reveal real events while rationalising harm. A character's mistaken belief is a canon fact about their belief, not proof of its content.

## Claim granularity

Keep one independently checkable proposition per evidence card. Avoid combining an observed action with a sweeping moral conclusion. Narcissa's lie after asking about Draco is an event; a complete political conversion is a separate proposition not established by it.

Record limits that materially change writing decisions. “Canon is complicated” is not useful; “this passage does not specify a penalty for refusing the Tournament” is.

## Verification status

The structured cards use `passage-checked` when the relevant context was read during development. The broader dossiers and references contain chapter-based interpretation and retrieval leads; they do not all carry that same status. Exact phrase locators help re-find evidence but a locator match alone does not verify the claim.

Chapter files and topic counts are machine-derived. They establish source structure and lexical occurrence, not understanding. The [coverage record](coverage.md) distinguishes these layers.

## Corrections and editions

Use chapter titles and anchors because page numbers vary. The importer records source hashes and language. If a source changes, regenerate the local catalogue and recheck decisive facts. Do not silently mix editions when a wording difference is the disputed point.

If primary passages appear to conflict, compare date, speaker, situation, later revelation and the narrowest claim both support. Preserve unresolved contradictions rather than inventing an explanatory law.

## Supplemental knowledge

Other media can be useful when the author selects them. Mark the exact adopted fact: a film action, an interview career detail, a game location or a stage-work event. Keep the seven-book account available so adaptation choices remain deliberate.

Real-world knowledge can enrich an invention: food preparation, regional language, material culture, work routines or historical context. Cite a reliable external source when precision matters. It does not turn a magical extension into canonical technology.

## Publication boundary

Public resources contain original analysis, small identifiers and chapter titles. Full texts and the local catalogue stay with the user's own books. The repository's license covers its original guidance and code, not the novels. A release should be checked by its actual file list, including generated files, rather than relying only on Git ignore rules.
