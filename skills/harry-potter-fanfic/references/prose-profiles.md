# Original prose profiles

Use when the brief asks for a particular reading experience, when a scene's tone feels generic, or when there is no established project voice. Approved project prose takes precedence. Choose only the controls that help the current scene; do not ask the user to fill out a questionnaire or present the profile unless useful.

## Controls and effects

| Control | Choices | Check in the draft |
|---|---|---|
| Narrative distance | Immediate perception; reflective closeness; deliberate wider narration | Does the chosen distance control what can be known and judged? |
| Humour | Absent; dry situational; playful; exuberant | Does the joke arise from this person's purpose or the situation, and can others answer it? |
| Dialogue pressure | Candid; evasive; bargaining; combative; companionable | Does a reply affect the next move rather than merely sound clever? |
| Rhythm | Spacious; conversational; compressed under pressure | Does sentence movement follow attention rather than a fixed length pattern? |
| Emotional explicitness | Mostly indirect; mixed; openly named | Is the feeling legible without explaining the same beat twice? |
| Imagery | Sparse; selective; lush | Does the image belong to this viewpoint and earn its space? |

These are independent controls, not quality rankings. Warmth can coexist with refusal; close narration can name a feeling; ornate prose can remain spatially clear. A danger scene can slow down around a difficult choice rather than become uniformly short sentences. No numerical quota for metaphors, jokes or dialogue improves every scene.

## Starting combinations

**Dry school comedy:** close or lightly reflective narration, situational humour, competing practical aims, conversational rhythm. Give an apparently minor inconvenience social consequences. Preserve sincere moments instead of making every sentence a punchline.

**Quiet emotional negotiation:** close narration, selective detail, mixed emotional explicitness, direct requests alongside what remains unsaid. Let care change a practical burden while leaving both people an independent purpose. Avoid making all characters sound like the same ideal counsellor.

**Constrained action:** immediate perception, clear geography at the hinge, rhythm that changes with available choices. Give mistakes persistent costs. A pause, a failed attempt or a mundane tool can matter without inventing an extra magical power.

**Investigative tension:** close or reflective narration, specific evidence, uncertain interpretation, dialogue shaped by what each person needs to establish. Keep the difference between what happened and what somebody claims happened. A useful ending may commit the investigator to an inconvenient next step without solving the mystery.

These combinations are starting points, not plot templates. They apply to original scenes in either language, not imitation of an author or translator. For Hungarian information order, particles and speech conventions, use the relevant sections of [Hungarian prose](hungarian-prose.md) and [terminology](hungarian-terminology.md).

## One targeted revision

After drafting, identify the passage where the intended experience weakens most. Is dialogue interchangeable, action hard to picture, emotion overexplained, or a transition unearned? Revise that passage at the appropriate level. Compare it with the original: preserve the distinctive line, awkward pause or unresolved feeling that was doing useful work.

Do not keep rewriting the whole scene until it conforms to every reference. Stop when the material defect is repaired, the brief holds, and another pass would mostly exchange defensible tastes. A stronger draft should remain this story, not become an example of the checklist.
