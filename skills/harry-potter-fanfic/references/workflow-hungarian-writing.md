# Hungarian writing workflow

Load for Hungarian drafting, continuation, translation of the user's own writing, or substantial Hungarian revision. This workflow complements [chapter production](workflow-chapter-production.md); it does not replace the project's continuity or create a second story bible.

Use [Hungarian prose](hungarian-prose.md) for narrative distance, rhythm, dialogue and register. Use [Hungarian terminology](hungarian-terminology.md) when names, inflection, address, spells or mixed-language terminology need attention. A spelling question does not require the prose reference; a Hungarian scene does not require reading six novels again.

For a short scene, this workflow and the accepted brief are enough to start. Open only the prose section that answers an actual voice question. Use [prose profiles](prose-profiles.md) for optional tone controls and [scene state](scene-state.md) when a handoff, private fact or physical limit matters. Read the long-chapter workflow only when the assignment needs its structure.

## Establish the writing contract

Recover these choices from the request and accepted manuscript before asking questions:

- Task: write a new scene, continue accepted prose, translate supplied original prose, revise Hungarian, or compare editions for research.
- Story state: era, canon cutoff, divergences, viewpoint, immediate previous event, relationships and present knowledge.
- Language: Hungarian narration and dialogue; project choices for foreign names, incantations, quoted documents and code-switching.
- Voice: narrative closeness, humour, emotional explicitness, sentence movement and age-appropriate vocabulary. Calibrate to the user's accepted original passages.
- Deliverable: target length, requested scene beats, format and existing save location.

Hungarian language does not relocate Hogwarts to Hungary. Hungarian customs, foods, school practices or family naming conventions need a character, setting or AU reason. A Hungarian original character can think in culturally specific terms without every British character acquiring the same habits.

If asked for an exact or near-exact living translator's style, briefly explain that you can provide original Hungarian prose with broad traits instead. Offer the useful traits already implied by the request—readable close narration, dry situational humour, precise dialogue, restrained emotion—and proceed with the authorized writing. Do not build a recognisability target, a translator fingerprint, a signature phrase collection or a sentence-by-sentence reskinning of a book passage.

## Separate the two kinds of evidence

English and Hungarian editions answer overlapping but different questions:

| Question | Evidence to inspect | What to carry into the draft |
|---|---|---|
| What happened, who witnessed it, what did someone know? | The relevant event in English, with surrounding context; compare the Hungarian passage if it introduces uncertainty | An event or knowledge constraint, with testimony distinguished from fact |
| What is this person, place or spell called in Hungarian? | The glossary record and its Hungarian source anchor | The verified term and its scope, subject to project override |
| How can this scene work in Hungarian? | Selected paired passages, read for function rather than wording | Broad choices about information order, register, rhythm and viewpoint |
| Does an unusual Hungarian detail establish new canon? | Both editions and, where needed, the actual source entry | A recorded discrepancy or unresolved question; not an invented reconciliation |

An English sentence boundary, pronoun, tense or idiom is not a Hungarian drafting instruction. Conversely, a fluent Hungarian rendering does not prove that a date, participant or magical rule is correct. Check numbers, names, negation, agency and modality before carrying a translation detail into continuity. Do not mistake a character's certainty for the narrator's confirmation.

For a project that deliberately follows a particular Hungarian edition, preserve that choice and label material differences in the existing continuity notes. Do not silently rewrite the chosen edition's facts or elevate a variant into universal canon.

## Retrieve by scene, then stop when the question is answered

Use the installed `scripts/hp.mjs` tools described in [tools](tools.md), with the user's actual source directories supplied through `--sources`. Keep English and Hungarian catalogues separate. Use the same chapter anchor in each language, then locate the same event; matching offsets, paragraph numbers and EPUB filenames are not reliable alignment.

For a narrow question, read enough before and after the match to identify speaker, addressee, cause and response. Expand when a revelation, negation or change of speaker affects interpretation. Keyword hits and catalogue chapter counts establish retrieval coverage, not semantic reading. The paired samples underlying this guidance are explicitly listed in [Hungarian prose](hungarian-prose.md#paired-evidence-sampling).

No local books are needed to use the original prose guidance. When evidence is unavailable, write from verified project facts and the glossary; flag an unresolved term only when it matters. Do not claim fresh passage verification. Keep source text and full-text catalogues outside the distributed skill, and never turn extracted passages into public examples.

## Build a scene that can support its language

Write a compact scene note in the project's existing format. For example:

> Nézőpont: egy harmadéves diák. Cél: visszakapni az elkobzott levelét. Akadály: a felügyelő tudni akarja, kinek vinné. A diák félreérti a kérdés célját. Fordulat: a barátja vállalja a kézbesítést, de cserébe választ kér a tegnapi hazugságra. A jelenet végére a levél útja megoldódik, a bizalom kérdése nyitva marad.

This is an original planning example, not a canon incident. It gives dialogue something to accomplish. Decide who can interrupt, refuse, misunderstand or change tactics. A magical obstacle needs an effect and a limit: a seal can identify an authorized recipient, for example, but that invented property must be established before it resolves the scene.

Choose the register from the current pressure. An early-school discovery can allow puzzled comparisons and social comedy; immediate danger calls for usable perception and decisions under strain. A late-era breakfast can still be funny. A first-year scene can still be frightening. Book number supplies context, not a mandatory emotional filter.

## Draft in Hungarian thought units

Draft from the scene note, without holding a source paragraph beside the cursor. Let the viewpoint character's attention determine what enters a sentence. Put the contrast where Hungarian emphasis needs it; do not repeatedly lead with a translated pronoun or preserve English clause order for its own sake.

When translating the user's own prose, first preserve the scene's information, uncertainty and relationships. Then reshape sentence boundaries, idioms and tags into natural Hungarian. Do not add a stronger accusation, a new joke, intimacy, or a magical ability merely because it makes the sentence easier to write. Explain a material adaptation when its meaning differs from the supplied text.

When expanding, add a choice, resistance, a new information route or aftermath. Avoid adding decorative enchantment to every object, extra synonyms for emotion, or repeated reassurance just to reach a word target. Let mundane tools, silence and unremarkable rooms remain available.

## Revise in three useful passes

1. **Story:** Who wants what? What causes the next action? Does a speaker know this? Did a translated detail change a fact? Are injuries, possessions and commitments still present at the end?
2. **Hungarian prose:** Read dialogue aloud. Inspect emphasis, unnecessary personal pronouns, tense handling, referents, speech tags and shifts between narrator judgment and character thought. Keep deliberate repetitions that carry pressure or comedy.
3. **Terminology and typography:** Check the glossary and project overrides, suffixes, name order, address relationships, accents, speech dashes and quoted incantations. Search foreign and Hungarian forms together, then review each hit in context.

Mechanical counts, duplicate detection and glossary matches cannot certify natural Hungarian, canon correctness or literary quality. If length matters, measure the saved draft using the host project's method and revise meaningful content toward the requested length.

Deliver the requested writing. Include only material unresolved decisions and verification limits in the handoff. Record new accepted terms or relationship changes in the existing project notes; this workflow does not authorize replacing those notes, installing tools, or publishing the manuscript.
