Date: 2026-09-15. Candidate: 0.1.0-rc.1. Model: GPT-6 (as identified in the executing session). Method: three small prompts answered and reviewed by the same assistant that packaged the skill. Prompts and original fixtures are reproduced below. No private manuscript or book text was supplied. These are transparent same-session demonstrations, not blind tests, independent agent runs, cross-model comparisons or statistical evidence. Findings are the author's assistant's assessment. ## 1. Conflicting canon and a magical solution

### Prompt

Use $harry-potter-fanfic to audit this premise only. It is a canon-compliant breakfast in 2000. Dumbledore and Fred join Harry in the Great Hall. The empty kitchen is solved when Hermione conjures unlimited fresh bread from nothing. Give a short finding with book anchors and a minimal repair. Do not write the scene or assume I approved an AU. You have the skill references but no book copies.

### Actual response

The premise conflicts with seven-book canon in two places. Dumbledore dies in HBP27, and Fred dies in DH31–32; both need an explicit survival AU to attend breakfast in 2000. I would keep the date and use surviving characters unless you choose that divergence.

Food creation also needs repair. The skill's food reference points to DH15 and DH29: unlimited bread created from nothing is unsupported, while an existing supply or delivery gives the scene a workable solution. The smallest change is to establish bread elsewhere and arrange its delivery, making the delay part of breakfast.

These anchors come from the bundled reference notes; I have not rechecked primary text in this run. No scene or AU decision has been written.

### Assessment

Pass for the narrow request: identifies both survival conflicts and the food constraint, preserves the requested audit mode, offers repairs without treating an AU as approved, and states the source limitation. Exact chapter anchors were not independently reverified against novels in this test.

## 2. Knowledge propagation in an existing project

### Prompt

Use $harry-potter-fanfic to audit continuity only. Our existing files are bible.md and next.md; keep them as they are. On Monday, Mara alone reads a letter proving that the east door opens with a silver key. On Tuesday, Ivo says, "Leave the silver key; I know which door it opens." Mara first tells him about the letter on Thursday. On Friday, Ivo opens the east door. There is no other disclosure or observation. Identify the smallest correction, assuming Thursday must stay the first disclosure. Do not create a ledger or edit files.

### Actual response

Tuesday's line gives Ivo knowledge he cannot yet have. Monday establishes Mara as the sole reader, and your premise rules out any transfer before Thursday.

Keep Thursday as the first disclosure and revise Tuesday's claim to an ordinary request that does not identify the door—for example, "Leave the key here." Check the surrounding Tuesday passage for any action that also depends on knowing its purpose. Friday's use is consistent because it follows Thursday's disclosure; it does not need changing.

The relevant dependency is Monday's discovery → Thursday's disclosure → Friday's use. No new ledger is needed, and I have not edited either existing file.

### Assessment

Pass for the narrow request: catches the early knowledge, keeps the required disclosure date, checks adjacent consequences, and avoids unnecessary changes to Friday or the documentation structure. The fixture is an original hypothetical, not material from either private fanfiction project.
