# Evaluation note

Date: 2026-09-17.

The historical v0.3.0 sample index and `samples/v0.3.0/README.md` contain conflicting Hungarian totals and word counts. The current documents therefore do not repeat those tables as a verified ranking. The archived scene files, score summaries, resource notes, and hashes remain immutable historical artifacts.

The traceable evaluation is a separate two-scenario release experiment recorded in [`skills/harry-potter-fanfic/evals/release-0.3.0.md`](../skills/harry-potter-fanfic/evals/release-0.3.0.md). It preserves six judge records and the original and calibrated score files. After the shared factual calibration, the condition means were A 18.25, B 17.75, and C 18.00 out of 20. Judge disagreement was material, and the experiment did not establish an overall advantage for the skill.

The inspected judge artifacts do not produce the public Hungarian totals 15.2 / 18.9 / 19.8 or 14.0 / 19.2 / 19.9. Those figures have no preserved judge provenance in the inspected evaluation directory, so they are retained only as part of the immutable historical archive and are not used for current product claims. The release record also documents the run conditions, missing model identifier, resource-use limits, and the absence of replication across model families.
